# Third-party implementation references

This project independently implements provider integrations after consulting the following MIT-licensed open-source projects and their documentation:

- [CodexBar](https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar) — provider authentication and usage data-source documentation, including Notion AI and Gemini CLI.
- [Unofficial Gemini Usage Tracker](https://github.com/Mohammad-Aali/Unofficial-Gemini-Usage-Tracker) — the approach of reading the rendered `gemini.google.com/usage` page and its English usage labels.
- [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) — provider logo vector paths (CC0-1.0 collection; OpenAI from v15, other available marks from v16). Individual brand marks remain subject to their owners' trademark and usage rules.
- [Grok](https://grok.com) — Grok mark taken from the service's own `images/favicon.svg` asset without changing its proportions.

The referenced services and product names are trademarks of their respective owners. This application is unofficial and is not endorsed by those service providers.
